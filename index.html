Maple Ridge Pottery Studio

We are a small pottery studio in Millbrook offering wheel-throwing classes and handmade ceramics.

About

Founded in 2015 by Dana Okafor, Maple Ridge Pottery Studio has taught over 3000 students. We specialize in stoneware mugs, hand-painted bowls, and custom wedding registries.
